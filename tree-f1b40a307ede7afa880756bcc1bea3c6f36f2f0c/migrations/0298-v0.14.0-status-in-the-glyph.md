# 0298 — Status lives in the glyph

Status color was painted across whole labels, rails and bands, so a screen
showing five states showed five colored sentences. The design language puts
status in the glyph cell and leaves the words in the body tone
(`docs/design/termrock-design-language.md` §3): color reinforces meaning, it
does not replace reading.

## Behavior changes

| Surface | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `SemanticStatus::Running` | `Role::Accent` | `Role::InfoDim` — running is live information, not the operator's current intent |
| `StatusIndicator` | whole label in the status role | label `Text`; the dot carries the status |
| `ActivityIndicator` | verb + spinner in one role, bold | verb `TextMuted`; the spinner cell carries the role |
| `Badge` | whole decorated string in the variant role | label `Text` over the same fill; the status glyph keeps the variant color |
| `Callout` (Section) | border painted in the tone | border `Role::Border`; tone stays on the rail and the glyph |
| `ErrorState` inline | glyph + summary one danger string | summary `TextStrong`; the glyph carries the kind |
| `Toast` | inner rail in the severity role | rail `Role::Border` through the glyph catalog; severity on the icon alone |
| `StatusBar` | `Mode` accent, `Selection` info, slot words in their kind role | slot words in `Role::StatusBar`; each slot's glyph carries its kind; `Mode` is `TextStrong` and `Selection` is `TextMuted` |

A slot or badge that sets an explicit style keeps it: the host's opinion still
wins over the default split.

## New internal helper

`widgets::row_chrome::paint_status_glyph(buffer, row, column, glyph, style)`
repaints one glyph cell after a row is painted, preserving whatever background
the row sits on so a selection wash survives. It is `pub(crate)`: the split is
a library-wide rule, not a consumer-facing API.

## Consumer edits

- Snapshots asserting a colored status *label* (log level words, badge text,
  status-bar slot text): the words are now neutral and the glyph carries the
  hue. Assert on the glyph cell.
- A host reading `SemanticStatus::role()` for `Running` gets `InfoDim`.
- `StatusSlotKind::default_role()` changed for `Mode` and `Selection`.

## Validation
