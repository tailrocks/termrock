- Example v2 instance: `docs/api/component-contracts.v2.example.json`

### Completeness law

A component is not complete because it compiles and renders. Applicable axes
must be `covered` with evidence, or `not_applicable` / `caller_owned` with
reason. `missing` / un-evidenced `covered` fail the future contracts gate.

### CI direction

1. Keep v1 catalog check.
2. Add `check-contracts` validating schema + evidence paths (phase Q1).
3. Migrate components to v2 axes; dual-accept during transition.
4. Design lints (hardcoded keys, color-only state) escalate to error.
