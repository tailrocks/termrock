## Key outcomes

`Delete` on a selected row emits `PickerOutcome::Secondary(id)` on the
initial press with no modifiers. The picker keeps the selected stable identity
and leaves the secondary action to the host. A missing, stale, disabled, or
non-navigable selection, repeat, release, or modified Delete produces
`PickerOutcome::Ignored`.

Hosts should route `Secondary(id)` to the row-level action appropriate for the
picker. The picker does not remove rows or close resources itself.
