# 0291 — One scrollbar language

Four scroll surfaces painted scrollbars four ways, in two visual languages, on
three copies of the same thumb math. `Tree` and `Form` drew `│` track with a
`█` thumb, `ScrollArea` drew `│`/`─` with `█` and derived thumb size and travel
by hand, `Viewport` kept a private copy of the canonical painter, and only
`List` used the shared renderer.

## Behavior change

Every scroll surface now paints the canonical language: a `·` track with a `┃`
vertical thumb and a `━` horizontal thumb, resolved from `Role::ScrollTrack`
and `Role::ScrollThumb`.

| Surface | Track before | Thumb before | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Tree` | `│` | `█` | `·` / `┃` |
| `Form` | `│` | `█` | `·` / `┃` |
| `ScrollArea` (vertical) | `│` | `█` | `·` / `┃` |
| `ScrollArea` (horizontal) | `─` | `█` | `·` / `━` |
| `TextArea` | via `ScrollArea` | via `ScrollArea` | follows `ScrollArea` |
| `Viewport` | `·` | `┃` | unchanged, now shared |

A gutter whose content fits stays blank instead of showing a full-height
thumb: `scroll::paint_list_scrollbar` paints nothing when the surface is not
scrollable.

## Ownership change

`scroll::paint_list_scrollbar` and `scroll::render_scrollbar` are the only
sanctioned scrollbar painters. Thumb size and travel come from
`scroll::full_cell_thumb` in one place; the three hand-rolled copies are gone.
Hit-rect bookkeeping stays at the call sites — `TreeState::scrollbar_region`,
`FormState::scrollbar_region`, and `TextAreaState`'s two track rects are
unchanged, so pointer paging and drag still resolve against the painted track.

## Consumer edits

- Snapshots or assertions pinning `█` or `│` on a `Tree`, `Form`, `ScrollArea`
  or `TextArea` scrollbar: re-bless to `┃` / `·` (`━` on the horizontal bar).
- A host that painted its own track next to one of these widgets for a
  consistent look should drop it; the widget now matches `List`.
