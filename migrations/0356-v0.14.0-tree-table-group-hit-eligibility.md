# 0356: TreeTable keeps group rows out of cursor selection

## Boundary

TreeTable group bands are structural rows, not navigable data rows. Their
disclosure hit remains actionable, but their body and context-menu hits must
not become selection or cursor authority.

## Change

TreeTable now shares one selectable-row predicate across reconciliation,
keyboard actions, semantic activation/toggle, and mouse hits. Mouse regions
also validate their projected row identity before routing, so a reprojected
slice cannot receive an action through stale hit geometry.

## Consumer impact

No consumer edit is required. Group bands remain expandable through their
disclosure hit; data-row selection and context actions are unchanged.
