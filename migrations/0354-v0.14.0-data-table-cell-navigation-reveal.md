# 0354: DataTable cell navigation reveals its cursor

## Boundary

Cell navigation may move to a column that was omitted from the initial
responsive projection or lies outside the current horizontal viewport.

## Change

DataTable expands the projection when necessary and adjusts the unpinned
horizontal offset until the cell cursor is painted. Row navigation keeps its
free horizontal-scroll behavior.

## Consumer impact

No consumer edit is required. Cell and range navigation now guarantee that
the active column remains represented by painted geometry after movement.
