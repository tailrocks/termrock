# 0439: StatusBar spacing and collision-free transient geometry

## Boundary

`StatusBar` uses plane spacing for persistent groups and items. It no longer
paints structural `·` separators, and a transient status sentence is placed in
the free span before the persistent right group instead of being painted
through that group. The change is visual and geometric; the public slot and
state APIs remain the same.

## Canonical evidence

The canonical implementation in
`/Users/donbeave/terminal-components-claude/src/widgets/statusbar.rs` defines
`GAP = 3` and `EDGE = 1`. Its layout starts the left group at `area.x + EDGE`,
ends the right group at `area.right() - EDGE`, accounts for item and group gaps
before priority admission, and centers the center group in the free span
between the two outer groups. Its module contract says groups are separated by
spacing and tone, not separator glyphs.

The canonical `DESIGN.md` Status bar section requires a full-width elevated
plane, left/center/right groups, and three cells of plane between groups and
items; it explicitly forbids `│` and `·` separators. The feedback contract at
`DESIGN.md` lines 202–204 and 1100–1104 gives the transient status ownership of
the right edge and requires hints or other lower-priority content to leave when
they would collide.

## Root cause

TermRock's `placements` path reserves three columns only for later items on
the same side, marks those allocations with `separator`, and
`StatefulWidget::render` paints `meta_separator()` (`·`) at
`placement.area.x + 1` before shifting content by three cells. This reuses a
structural divider where the canonical widget requires blank elevated cells.
The placement budget also does not model the canonical one-cell outer edges
and explicit gaps between groups.

`paint_transient` computes `occupied_right` from the right placements, but
discards it when calculating the label origin: it always uses
`area.right() - width - 1`. The guard compares `occupied_right` with a left
boundary unrelated to that origin. Because persistent right slots are painted
at the same right edge, a transient label can overlap their cells.

## Exact geometry and behavior

- Fill the requested row with the status-bar elevated-plane style.
- Reserve one plane cell at each outer edge. The first left cell is at
  `area.x + 1`; the last right content cell ends at `area.right() - 1`.
- Reserve exactly three blank plane cells between retained items and between
  retained groups. These cells are budgeted before deciding which lower-
  priority slots leave. They contain no `·`, `│`, or replacement glyph.
- Keep the canonical narrow-row policy: remove the lowest-priority item first,
  with center before right before left on ties; the strongest left item
  survives and truncates rather than disappearing. Slot content, chip padding,
  meters where supported, hover styles, hit regions, and semantic identities
  remain attached to their slot rectangles.
- Place left items left-to-right, right items right-to-left, and center items
  centered in the free span after the outer groups and their three-cell gaps.
  Item widths include rendered glyphs; spacing never consumes a retained
  item's content width.
- Build a transient label from its optional glyph, one separating space, and
  its text. Let `collision_right` be the left edge of the nearest retained
  right slot, or `area.right()` when no right slot exists. Place the transient
  at `collision_right - width - 1`, leaving one plane cell before the right
  boundary. If the available span cannot hold the label, omit or truncate it
  according to the existing transient-width policy; never write into a right
  slot.
- The transient remains non-interactive and does not create a hit region or
  semantic identity. Persistent right slots keep their exact order and
  ownership when a transient is present.
- No caller-side padding, separator insertion, or alternate rendering path is
  added. Every recipe and row width uses this same geometry.

## P1 parity decisions

- **Truncation:** included. Retained contracted slot content uses the shared
  grapheme-safe end truncator with `…`; wide glyphs and combining sequences are
  never split.
- **Priority/layout:** included. Existing `StatusSlot::priority` values keep
  their public `u8` API, while eviction follows canonical relative ordering:
  lowest priority first, then center/right/left ties, with the strongest left
  slot as the truncating survivor. The center group is centered in its free
  span.
- **Plane/semantics:** included in this widget. The row fills with
  `Role::Elevated`; each item keeps its semantic role/tone on that plane, and
  hover lifts to the overlay plane with primary text. The global
  `Role::StatusBar` palette contract remains unchanged for other consumers.
- **Deferred canonical fields:** canonical `StatusItem` also exposes explicit
  `Emphasis::{Strong,Chip}` and inline meters. Local `StatusSlot` has no
  corresponding fields (`status_bar.rs` lines 174–194), and adding a second
  public API is outside this geometry/collision migration. Existing local
  glyph, semantic, hover, hit-region, and recipe behavior remains intact.
- **Local transient extension:** canonical StatusBar has no transient field.
  TermRock retains its existing public transient API, now collision-safe; an
  over-constrained transient is omitted rather than introducing a new
  truncation policy.

## Validation

Update focused `StatusBar` tests to prove the complete contract:

- two or more same-side slots contain their full content while the three-cell
  gap contains only the bar plane and the rendered row contains no `·` or `│`;
- wide and narrow rows retain one-cell outer edges, include the three-cell
  group gap when both groups survive, and apply priority dropping/truncation
  without letting spacing eat content;
- center placement is centered in the space between the left and right
  groups;
- a transient with no right slots keeps the one-cell right margin, while a
  transient with right slots ends before `collision_right` and never shares a
  cell with a persistent slot; an over-constrained row does not paint it;
- clickable regions still cover only persistent slot rectangles and transient
  text remains non-interactive.

## Compatibility impact

This is a visual and layout breaking change. Golden frames and tests that
expect `·` or `│`, zero outer padding, old group/item coordinates, or a
transient overlaid on right slots must be updated. Narrow layouts may drop or
truncate a transient earlier because persistent right slots now own their
collision boundary. The public `StatusBar`, `StatusSlot`, state, event, hit,
and semantic APIs do not require caller source changes.

There is no compatibility shim, separator toggle, legacy geometry mode, alias,
or caller opt-in. Consumers must not reintroduce the old glyphs or add manual
padding. No persisted or serialized data changes; rollback, if temporarily
needed for an older visual baseline, is a source-revision rollback.
