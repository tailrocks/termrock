# 0308 — Overlay hints share one vocabulary

`HintBar` and the `HintKey`/`HintText`/`HintSeparator` role trio existed for
footer hints, and one overlay of twenty-three used them. The rest joined their
chords into a flat sentence by hand, each picking its own separator, its own
tone, and its own behaviour when the row ran out of width.

## Behavior changes

Seven overlays now paint their footer through `HintBar`: `CommandPalette`,
`HistoryPicker`, `NotificationCenter`, `QuestionFlow`, `ProgressSteps`,
`AlertDialog`, and `Dialog` itself. They share one separator from the glyph
catalog (` · `, ` | ` under ASCII), one alignment path, and one contraction
rule — an over-wide hint row ends in the catalog ellipsis instead of a hard
cut.

Chord copy is now sentence-consistent as a side effect: `esc cancel` and
`esc restore draft` became `esc close`, and `[] step` / `f full` left the
default rows (the chords still work; the full map belongs to the keyboard-help
overlay).

`NotificationCenter` no longer swaps to a spaced-out `"j/k enter u x c / esc"`
under ASCII — the ASCII form comes from the glyph profile, not from a second
string.

## API changes

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| `NOTIFICATION_CENTER_HINT: &str` | `NOTIFICATION_CENTER_HINTS: &[Hint<'static>]` |
| `PROGRESS_STEPS_HINT: &str` | `PROGRESS_STEPS_HINTS: &[Hint<'static>]` |
| — | `Dialog::hints(&[Hint])` |

`Dialog::footer_hint(&str)` stays for plain copy that is not a chord list;
`hints()` is the structured path and takes precedence when both are set.
`HistoryPicker::footer_hint` and `CommandPalette::footer_hint` still accept a
host override string, and now default to `None` — the widget's own chords are
the fallback.

## Consumer edits

- Replace `NOTIFICATION_CENTER_HINT` / `PROGRESS_STEPS_HINT` with the `_HINTS`
  slices, or build your own `&[Hint]`.
- Snapshots of any of the seven footers: separators and copy changed.
