# 0364: TreeTable reconciliation removes invalid checked rows

## Boundary

Multi-selection must not retain IDs that the current projection now marks as
disabled or otherwise non-selectable.

## Change

TreeTable reconciliation removes checked IDs for non-selectable rows present in
the refreshed projection before repairing the primary cursor and selection.

## Consumer impact

Off-window checked IDs remain intact for virtualized hosts. IDs represented by a
disabled, loading, group, or aggregate row are removed during reconciliation.
