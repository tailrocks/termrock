# 0287 — Light-preset contrast and the faint tier

The contrast floor (migration 0283) recorded twenty-five pairs that could not
clear it. The graphite ladder (0284) retired the phosphor ones. This retires
the light-preset ones and closes the gap the new `TextFaint` role opened.

## Value changes

`RolePalette::paper()` — status hues were picked against a web page, not
against paper, and measured 2.77–4.10 against their own canvas:

## Floor table additions

- `TextFaint` on Canvas and Surface must clear 3.0 (7.0 in `high_contrast`).
- New test `disabled_and_faint_tiers_stay_distinguishable`: the two quiet
  tiers must differ by at least 0.4 ratio points against the canvas in every
  preset.
- `floor_table_covers_every_text_tier` replaces a role-count tripwire that a
  same-size role swap walked straight past. It now asserts the table names
  every text tier and every surface.

## Reported: the ladder and the hairline cannot both hold

Nine ladder pairs stay in `KNOWN_SHORTFALLS`, and not because a value is
wrong. A ladder stepping 1.15 per rung spans 1.52 from Canvas to Elevated; a
single `Border` tone that stays 1.3 clear of *both* ends needs 1.52 ≥ 1.69.
The two rows of the floor table are mutually unsatisfiable while one border
role serves every elevation.

Measured candidate, for whoever takes the design call: Canvas `#0a0c0a` →
Surface `#1f2321` → Raised `#2d2f2a` → Elevated `#393d3b` steps 1.20 per rung
and spans 1.73, which leaves room for a mid-tone border — but it needs a
second border tone (or a per-elevation border) to keep dialog hairlines
visible, which is a palette-identity decision, not a value fix.
`high_contrast` is a separate case: it paints Surface *on* Canvas on purpose —
one black ground, maximum text contrast, structure carried by borders.

## Consumer edits

- Pinning any paper or slate role value listed above: re-pin.
- A custom palette that reused `TextDisabled` for `TextFaint`: give the faint
  tier its own value, at least 0.4 ratio points away.
