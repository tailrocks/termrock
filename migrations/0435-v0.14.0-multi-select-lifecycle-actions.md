# 0435: MultiSelect lifecycle actions

## Boundary

`MultiSelectState::handle_key` treats activation, dismissal, confirmation, and
membership mutations as physical key-press actions. A held key must not
repeatedly open a closed multi-select, close it, or change its checked set.

## Change

- Press Enter, Space, Down, or closed-state typeahead opens a focused
  MultiSelect; Repeat and Release are ignored at the closed boundary.
- Press Backspace/Delete clears a closed checked set; Repeat and Release are
  ignored.
- Press Escape closes an open MultiSelect; Press Ctrl+A selects visible
  options; Press Ctrl+D/Ctrl+Backspace clears checks; Repeat and Release do
  not repeat those mutations.
- Press Space toggles the highlighted option and Press Enter confirms/closes.
  Repeat and Release are ignored for those actions when Space is a toggle;
  searchable Space remains repeatable query text once the query is non-empty.
- Press Ctrl+U clears the searchable query to its start; Repeat and Release are
  ignored before that text-input mutation. Ordinary search editing, typeahead
  after opening, navigation, scrolling, and shift-range navigation retain
  their repeatable behavior.

## Host migration

Hosts routing raw MultiSelect events must continue passing the complete
`KeyEvent`, including its lifecycle kind. Do not synthesize repeated open,
close, or membership outcomes for Repeat or Release events; the state adapter
consumes them as no-ops.
