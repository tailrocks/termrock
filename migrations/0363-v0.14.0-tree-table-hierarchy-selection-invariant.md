# 0363: TreeTable hierarchy entry skips non-selectable rows

## Boundary

Hierarchy entry must preserve the same selectable-row contract as ordinary
cursor movement and reconciliation. Enabled group bands are painted rows, but
they are not valid cursor or selection targets.

## Change

When Right enters the first descendant of an expanded TreeTable branch, it now
skips group bands and other non-selectable rows and enters the first selectable
descendant.

## Consumer impact

No consumer edit is required. Hierarchy entry may now skip a group band before
selecting the first data or aggregate descendant.
