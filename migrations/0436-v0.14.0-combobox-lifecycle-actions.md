# 0436: Combobox lifecycle actions

## Boundary

`ComboboxState::handle_key` treats menu activation, dismissal, commit/create,
and host-facing text chords as physical key-press actions. A held key must not
repeatedly open or close the menu, commit/create a value, reset a draft, or
repeat a clipboard request.

## Change

- Press Escape dismisses or closes the menu; Press Enter commits, creates, or
  begins editing; Press Tab/BackTab commits an active suggestion when handled
  by the combobox. Repeat and Release are ignored for these lifecycle paths.
- Press Down opens a closed menu; Repeat and Release are ignored at the closed
  boundary. Open-menu arrows and paging retain repeatable navigation.
- Non-Press Ctrl+M is ignored before the TextInput submit fallback. Non-Press
  Ctrl+U/K/W and Ctrl+C/X/V are ignored before destructive draft or clipboard
  fallbacks. Ordinary printable text and Backspace/Delete editing remain
  repeatable.
- DraftChanged is emitted only when draft text changes; caret or selection-only
  edits do not start a suggestion generation or host fetch. Clipboard cut
  retains its cut outcome instead of being reported as copy.
- Completion-menu activation remains Press-only; stale-generation handling and
  host-owned suggestion fetching are unchanged.

## Host migration

Hosts routing raw Combobox events must continue passing the complete
`KeyEvent`, including its lifecycle kind. Do not synthesize repeated menu,
commit, create, dismiss, or clipboard outcomes for Repeat or Release events;
the state adapter consumes them as no-ops.
