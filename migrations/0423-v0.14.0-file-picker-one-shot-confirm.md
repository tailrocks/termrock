# 0423: FilePicker one-shot raw-key actions

## Boundary

`FilePickerState::handle_key` distinguishes one-shot actions from repeatable
navigation at the raw keyboard-event boundary. A held key must not repeat a
confirmation, selection toggle, cancellation, or filter/mode transition.

## Change

For FilePicker-owned actions, the contract is:

- `Press` Escape leaves Path/Preview or cancels the picker; `Repeat` and
  `Release` are ignored.
- `Press` Enter opens the highlighted directory or confirms the highlighted
  file; `Repeat` and `Release` are ignored.
- `Press` Space toggles the active item in multi-select mode; `Repeat` and
  `Release` are ignored.
- `Press` Ctrl+H toggles hidden entries; `Repeat` and `Release` are ignored.
- `Press` Tab and Ctrl+L switch pane focus; `Repeat` and `Release` are
  ignored.

Collection movement and hierarchy navigation remain repeatable. Direct
`open_highlight`, `handle_intent`, and mouse APIs remain phase-free because
they do not receive raw key-event lifecycle information.

## Host migration

Hosts routing raw FilePicker events must pass the complete `KeyEvent` to
`FilePickerState::handle_key` and treat `Ignored` as a no-op. Do not invoke
`open_highlight` or host-side dismissal a second time for Repeat or Release.

Path-pane Enter is delegated to `PathInputState`/`TextInputState` and retains
its own migration boundary; this migration does not alter nested text-input
submission.
