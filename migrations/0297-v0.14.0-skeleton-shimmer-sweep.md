# 0297 — Skeleton sweeps instead of pulsing

A skeleton says "this shape is coming". A pulse says "work is happening at this
rate" — that is a spinner's job, and conflating them is why loading states read
as busy noise (`docs/design/tui-motion-system.md` §6, and the "skeleton never
spins" line in §7's anti-patterns).

`Skeleton` used to alternate its *whole block* between `TextDisabled` and
`TextMuted` every 400 ms. It now paints a raised-cosine band travelling across
the shape, sampled per column from `style::shimmer_at`.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| `SKELETON_PULSE_PERIOD_MS` (400) | `SKELETON_SHIMMER_PERIOD_MS` (1500) |
| `SkeletonState::pulse_phase(tick, motion) -> u8` | *removed* |
| Whole block alternates two roles | Band sweeps left to right, peak capped at `style::AMBIENT_PEAK` (0.33) |

Consumers reading `pulse_phase` to drive their own chrome should take the
sweep instead — `style::shimmer_at(policy, elapsed_ms, col, cols, period)` is
the same primitive the widget uses, and `style::shimmer_cells` gives a whole
row.

The redraw contract is unchanged: shimmer stays **opt-in** via
`SkeletonState::set_shimmer(true)`, `should_tick` still requires
`MotionPolicy::Full`, and a default skeleton is inert and schedules no frames.

Under `Basic` the band parks at its bright end and under `Off` it is dark, so
two ticks render identical cells at either tier —
`design_gate::motion_policy_off_is_static` covers it.

## Why the period grew

400 ms is spinner cadence; at that speed a sweep reads as flicker. §6 asks for
~1.5 s, which is slow enough to register as ambient rather than as progress.

## Ownership and rollback

TermRock owns the sweep; hosts still decide whether a skeleton shimmers at all.
Rollback is source-only.

## Validation

```sh
cargo nextest run -p termrock --lib widgets::skeleton
cargo nextest run -p termrock --test design_gate
```
