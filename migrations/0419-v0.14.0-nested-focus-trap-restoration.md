# 0419: Nested focus traps restore their outer boundary

## Boundary

Focus trapping is stack-scoped. Closing an inner modal trap must restore the
outer trap before focus navigation or programmatic focus resumes.

## Change

`FocusGraph` now stores each trap root with its opener. Popping a nested trap
restores the previous root instead of clearing trapping entirely, while the
existing opener restoration behavior remains unchanged.

## Consumer impact

Consumers may nest modal focus traps without manually reapplying the outer
root after dismissing an inner trap. Background focus requests remain ignored
until the outermost trap is popped.
