# 0378 — v0.14.0 Menu input phases and buffer clipping

## Boundary

`MenuBar` and `DropdownMenu` consume raw `KeyEvent` phases for keyboard
navigation and activation. Their open cascades share the menu-row model, but
the active typeahead state belongs to the currently focused cascade panel.
`MenuBar::paint` also receives caller rectangles that can be offset from, or
outside, the Ratatui buffer.

## Change

### DropdownMenu and ContextMenu

- `PageUp` and `PageDown` route to `UiIntent::Page` and move the active row by
  the collection page size. Page movement clears the active panel's typeahead.
- `DropdownMenuState::typeahead_buffer()` reports the buffer of the active
  cascade frame. Opening a submenu starts that frame with an empty buffer;
  navigation, activation, and layer changes clear the frame they leave.
- Enter, Space, Escape, and a valid horizontal cascade transition act only on
  a single `KeyEventKind::Press`. Repeat and release events return
  `Ignored` before they can reconcile or mutate the cascade. Up/down,
  Home/End, PageUp/PageDown, and printable typeahead remain repeatable where
  their normal key policy permits it. `h`/`l` remain typeahead when the
  focused row has no cascade transition.
- `handle_intent` remains the semantic entry point. It has no physical key
  phase; hosts that call it directly still control how often the intent is
  supplied.

### MenuBar and its open cascade

- Mnemonic-mode toggling, top-level and panel mnemonic activation, and the
  narrow CommandPalette preference require a single key press. The default
  menu-bar bridge likewise emits Enter, Space, and Escape intents only for a
  press; navigation remains repeatable.
- A focused, closed full or compact bar paints the selection gutter in the
  leading cell or inter-label gap. Open or unfocused bars do not paint that
  closed-bar gutter. `MenuBarState::handle_mouse` remains the pointer entry
  point after the leading inset.
- `MenuBar::paint` intersects its input rectangle with `buffer.area()` before
  painting or publishing bar hits. Partial offset rectangles publish only
  in-buffer hits; empty or disjoint intersections clear hits and perform no
  writes.

## Host migration

1. Preserve `KeyEventKind` when routing raw keys to `handle_key`. Treat
   `Ignored` from repeat/release activation, dismissal, mnemonic, or cascade
   transitions as a no-op; do not synthesize a press for those phases.
2. Continue routing held navigation and typeahead events. Do not apply a
   blanket press-only filter to all menu keys.
3. Read `typeahead_buffer()` as the active panel's diagnostic state. Do not
   maintain a second aggregate buffer across cascade frames.
4. Route bar pointer events through `MenuBarState::handle_mouse` and pass the
   normal bar layout rectangle to `MenuBar::paint`; the bar clips against the
   target buffer.

## Verification

The menu tests cover page movement, active-frame typeahead, repeat/release
guards for lifecycle and cascade actions, mnemonic and CommandPalette press
guards, closed focused-bar gutter placement, and partial or disjoint bar
painting against offset buffers.

## Rollback

Rollback is source-only: restore the pre-0378 menu widget revision and keep
host routing unchanged. No persisted data or external migration is involved.
