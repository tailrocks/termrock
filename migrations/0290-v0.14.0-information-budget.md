# 0290 — The information budget

Patterns showed everything at once: a status header packed nine segments in
five hues into one permanent row, a session preview ran to twelve lines, a
permission prompt listed a dozen meta lines above its decisions, and footer
rows advertised up to nine chords. Law §4.2 now states the budget and two
gates measure it.

## The rule (docs/design/web-premium-tui-law.md §4.2)

A default frame shows ≤3 content zones, spends ≤1 hint row carrying ≤5 chords,
keeps metadata to ≤⅓ of visible rows, and speaks in ≤8 content hues — a hue
carrying a status *glyph* is not a content hue. Everything else is one keypress
away **and its affordance is visible in the frame**. Detail is never deleted,
only moved. Safety information does not bend to the budget; charts are exempt
from the hue count.

## Behavior changes

| Surface | Default frame now | Behind one keypress |
|---|---|---|
| `AgentStatusHeader` | work glyph+verb, connection *only when not ready*, model, non-empty queue, `i +N` | branch, mode, context, cost, elapsed (`i`) |
| `SessionPicker` preview | title, when, model, status glyph+word, summary, `i details` | project, branch, location, mode, device, pins, unread, action-required (`i`) |
| `SessionPicker` rows | status paints its glyph cell; the title is neutral | — |
| `PermissionPrompt` | actor+risk, operation→target, command, egress destination, `reversible: NO`, warning, decisions, `d details` | target annotation, run location, accessed data, non-egress destination, `reversible: yes`, expected result, prior grant (`d`) |
| `ConnectionManager` rows | status glyph, name, env | target, protocol, kind (detail pane) |
| `MetricsDashboard` toolbar | title, range, live/paused, comparison *when set*, failed count | refresh cadence and layout mode (visible in the frame itself; `C-k cmds`) |

`PermissionPrompt` keeps every safety fact in the collapsed frame: what leaves
the machine, what cannot be undone, the warning, and the command itself. Only
reassurance (`reversible: yes`) and provenance move.

Ten pattern footers dropped to five advertised chords: `auth_entry`,
`background_task_panel`, `error_recovery`, `file_manager`, `git_workbench`,
`help_center`, `metrics_dashboard`, `project_launcher`, `prompt_queue` (both
rows), `query_editor`, `session_picker`, `connection_manager`. The unadvertised
chords still work; the full map belongs to the keyboard-help overlay.

`AgentStatusHeader` binds `i` to the segment sheet. The plan asked for `s`;
`s` already opens sessions, and `i` matches `SessionPicker`'s details toggle.

## New state

`SessionPickerState::preview_details` and
`AgentStatusHeaderState::segments_expanded` are public `bool` fields, default
`false`. A host that builds either state with a struct literal must add them.

## Gates

- `design_gate.rs::pattern_style_diversity` — ≤8 hues that paint a word, over
  five priority patterns rendered with their own fixtures.
- `design_gate.rs::pattern_hint_budget` — one footer hint row per frame.
- `design_gate.rs::pattern_hint_copy_budget` — source scan: no painted hint
  literal in `patterns/` advertises more than five chords.

## Measured before → after (phosphor, 72×18)

Hues counted are those painting a word, not a glyph; frames rendered at 72×18
with each pattern's own `example_*` fixture.

| Surface | Content hues | Footer chords |
|---|---|---|
| `agent_status_header` | 5 → 5 (segments 9 → 4) | — |
| `session_picker` | 9 → 6 | 7 → 5 |
| `connection_manager` | 7 → 7 (row facts 5 → 3) | 9 → 5 |
| `plan_review` | 6 (untouched) | — |
| `integration_status` | 5 (untouched) | — |

`connection_manager` keeps seven hues because its detail pane still paints
status words; `plan_review` and `integration_status` were already inside the
budget. The measurable win in both dieted rows is facts per row, and in
`session_picker` a three-hue drop from moving status into the glyph cell.

## Consumer edits

- Struct-literal construction of the two states above: add the new field.
- A host relying on `SessionPicker`'s preview or `PermissionPrompt`'s frame to
  show every field by default: set `preview_details` / `details_expanded` to
  `true`, or advertise the toggle as the widget now does.
- Snapshots of any dieted surface: re-bless.
