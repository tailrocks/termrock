# 0376 — v0.14.0 Panel/Card surface resolution

## Boundary

`Panel::new` and `Card::new` now own a filled card surface by default. Quiet
panels resolve to `SurfaceRecipe::Inset`, paint the `Role::Surface` plane, and
keep the canonical `2×1` card inset. Panel and Card paint entry points clip
their requested rectangles to the Ratatui buffer before layout or cell writes.
Tiny rectangles keep the inset and use saturating geometry instead of falling
back to a full-area body.

The resolver also makes the public surface recipe and the rendered plane agree
for the variants that own a frame or overlay. Focus and danger change chrome;
they do not promote an in-flow panel to a different surface plane.

This follows the current `DESIGN.md` plane contract: cards use the filled
Surface plane, framed in-flow panes use Canvas, and dialogs or popups use the
elevated plane. Migration 0103 preserves Panel/Card anatomy and the `raised`
builder; this boundary clarifies which plane that preserved API paints.

## Surface contract

`PanelRecipe.surface` remains the semantic surface style selected by the
panel. The effective background is capability-aware and is resolved through
the existing `Surface::plan` policy. A monochrome projection may therefore
skip a chromatic fill or use the Canvas fallback while the semantic recipe
continues to identify Surface, Canvas, or Elevated. No `PanelRecipe.fill`
field is added.

The effective planes are:

| Construction | Surface recipe | Painted plane |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet / Card | `Inset` | Surface, filled |
| Bordered, Interactive, or Selected in flow | `Canvas` | Canvas, framed where applicable |
| Non-divider overlay | Overlay family | Elevated, framed |
| DividerOnly | `Canvas` | Transparent unless legacy `raised(true)` is selected |

Selected membership supersedes the older 0103 Selection-fill wording. A
Selected in-flow Panel remains on the Canvas plane and states membership with
its selection gutter; focus remains a separate chrome state.

## DividerOnly precedence

DividerOnly owns the geometry boundary and never receives an overlay frame.
The precedence rules are explicit:

1. Ordinary DividerOnly leaves existing buffer backgrounds untouched while it
   paints its divider rules.
2. The retained `.raised(true)` API opts DividerOnly into the ordinary filled
   Surface plane. It still does not create a frame.
3. `DividerOnly + raised(true) + overlay(true)` keeps DividerOnly precedence:
   it paints the filled Surface plane without an overlay frame. The overlay
   flag cannot turn a divider region into a modal surface.

Non-divider `.overlay(true)` forces a frame and selects the matching elevated
overlay recipe. The public `.raised(bool)`, `Panel::elevation()`,
`PanelRecipe` shape, and owned/borrowed Panel and Card `Widget` implementations
remain source-compatible in this migration. No public API removal is part of
this boundary.

## Host migration

Hosts that relied on a default or Quiet Panel being transparent must choose
`PanelVariant::DividerOnly` explicitly. Hosts may continue using existing
`Panel::raised` and `Panel::elevation` calls; rendering is driven by the
resolved surface recipe rather than by a separate elevation hint.

No Card fill override is needed. Card descriptions inherit the effective
surface background, including monochrome fallback behavior, while transparent
DividerOnly composition preserves seeded cells.

## Verification contract

Tests cover default and Card fills, semantic recipe versus capability-specific
effective fill, Canvas selection planes and gutters, DividerOnly precedence,
partial and disjoint buffer rectangles, tiny inset geometry, monochrome
projection, danger title markers, and owned/borrowed Panel Widget rendering.
