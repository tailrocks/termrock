# 0318 — Tables say where they are cut

The craft pass's remaining geometry work, which waited on the row-chrome
rewrites in plans 005/006/012.

## Behavior changes

**A horizontally scrolled `DataTable` says so.** Columns simply stopped at the
clip edge, with nothing to say more existed. The header's edge cells now carry
`‹` when the view is scrolled right of the first column and `›` when columns
remain off the right edge, both in `TextFaint`.

**Column separators come from the glyph catalog.** `DataTable` and `TreeTable`
each carried a private `const SEP: &str = "│"`, so an ASCII profile got a
Unicode box-drawing character anyway. Both route through
`GlyphSet::rule_v()`.

**Menu separators meet their border.** A `DropdownMenu` separator ran the inner
width and stopped one cell short of the panel's frame, leaving a notch on both
sides. It meets the border with `├` and `┤` (`+` under ASCII).

**Form columns follow density.** `COLUMN_GAP: u16 = 2` became
`system.spacing.gap`: a dashboard-density form no longer carries a comfortable
form's gutters.

## New surface

```rust
use termrock::style::Glyph;

let tee = system.glyphs.resolve(Glyph::RuleTeeLeft).text;  // ├ / +
```

`Glyph::RuleTeeLeft` and `Glyph::RuleTeeRight` join the chrome group of the
catalog, so they degrade with every other rule glyph.

## Consumer edits

- Snapshots of a scrolled `DataTable` header gain edge chevrons.
- Snapshots of a `DropdownMenu` with separators gain tees at the frame.
- Snapshots of a two-column `Form` at non-default density: the gutter follows
  the density now.
