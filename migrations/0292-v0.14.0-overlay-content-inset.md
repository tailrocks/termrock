# 0292 — Bordered overlays reserve their gutter

Nine bordered overlays painted their content flush against the border glyph:
they built their surface with `padding(0, 0)` and then wrote at `inner.x`, so
the first character of every row sat directly on `│`. `Callout` did it twice —
its severity rail landed on the border column and read as a double rule.

## Behavior change

`Drawer`, `DropdownMenu`, `NotificationCenter`, `PreviewCard`, `Popover`,
`MenuBar`, `FullscreenViewer`, `ImageSurface`, and `Callout` now reserve one
column on each side of their content, at every density and every width. Their
content rect — and therefore every hit rect and slot derived from it — moves in
by one column, and the usable content width shrinks by two.

Vertical geometry is unchanged: a border already separates rows, so bordered
chrome insets no rows.

## New surface

```rust
use termrock::widgets::{Surface, SurfaceRecipe};

let system = termrock::style::DesignSystem::phosphor();
let inner = Surface::new(&system)
    .recipe(SurfaceRecipe::Overlay)
    .bordered(true)
    .content_inset() // was: .padding(0, 0)
    .layout(area)
    .content;
```

`Surface::content_inset()` resolves `DesignSystem::content_inset(bordered)`.
It is a no-op on a borderless surface: there is no glyph to clear, so the
caller's own layout keeps owning that spacing.

## Gates

- `design_gate.rs::text_never_touches_borders` — for the bordered surface
  recipes, at every width from 3 to 39, content starts at least one column
  inside the border and ends at least one column before it.
- `design_gate.rs::bordered_overlays_reserve_their_gutters` — source scan: no
  member of the overlay family builds its surface with `padding(0, 0)`.

## Consumer edits

- Snapshots of any of the nine overlays: re-bless; content shifted one column
  right and wrapped one column earlier.
- A host that added its own one-cell margin inside one of these overlays to
  compensate should drop it, or its content will sit two columns in.
- Layout math that assumed `inner.width == area.width - 2` for these widgets
  now needs `- 4`. Prefer reading the rect the widget exports.
